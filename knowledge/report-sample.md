# Reflective report — sample output

Illustrative mocks in the chosen voice (**warmer · sparing · spare**). Numbers are
hand-set here; the build computes them in code and hands them to the model. Here to
judge voice and shape before wiring the AI.

---

## Sample — end of the 30

**Thirty days.**

You started heavy. You didn't stay there — more of your recent days landed steady
than your early ones did.

Early word: *tired*. Lately: *calm*, *content*. Intensity, 7 → just under 5.

On the days you sat ten minutes, the next morning ran lower. On the days you didn't,
it didn't. Worth testing on purpose.

Try: settle before your hardest block, on your hardest day. Watch the next morning.

What made the steady days steady?

*30 check-ins, 22 days. It can't see the rest. Seen, not scored.*
