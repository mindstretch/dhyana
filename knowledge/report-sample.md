# Reflective report — sample output

Illustrative mocks in the chosen voice (**warmer · sparing quotes**), from realistic
seeded data. The numbers here are hand-set; in the build they're computed in code and
handed to the model. This is here to judge the *voice and shape* before wiring the AI.

---

## Sample — end of the 30

**These thirty days.**

You started heavy, and you didn't stay there. It wasn't a clean line — there were hard
days right to the end — but the direction is real: more of your recent days landed
steady than your early ones did.

Early on, the word was *tired*. It showed up more than any other, usually trailing a
long workday. By the last week, *calm* and *content* were turning up about as often as
the hard ones. Your intensity walked the same path — it began around 7 and eased to
just under 5. Not gone. Lower.

You're also naming things more precisely than you were three weeks ago — "overwhelmed,"
"wired," instead of just "off." That sounds small. It isn't. Naming a feeling clearly
is part of how it loosens.

One pattern worth noticing: on the days you sat for ten minutes or more, the next
morning's intensity was lower more often than not. On the days you skipped, it wasn't.
That's not proof — it's your own data leaning one way, and it's worth testing on purpose.

Work threaded through the reflections more than anything else — deadlines, the feeling
of being *on*. One night you just wrote, "too much, again." Twice, though, you wrote
about your wife in a way that read lighter than the rest.

You circled *scattered* a few times this month. Your own capstone made the case that
scattered isn't a character flaw — it's a nervous system that hasn't been given rest.
The Restoration practice you built points exactly there.

**One thing to try.** Next stretch: sit ten minutes on your hardest workday, before the
hardest block — and note the next morning. You have a hunch in your data. Make it an
experiment.

**A question to sit with.** What made the steady days steady? You have twelve of them
to look back on now.

*This reads 30 check-ins across 22 days. It can't see the days you didn't check in, and
it won't pretend to. Seen, not scored.*

## Sample — weekly

**This week** was steadier than last — three of seven days landed regulated, where last
week only one did.

The days you sat, your intensity ran lower than the days you didn't. Small sample, but
it's leaning.

What would it take to protect the two good mornings you had this week?

---

**Note on quoting:** "sparing" in practice = one short phrase from your own reflections
per report, max, and only when it earns its place. Everything else is paraphrase, so
the report never feels like it's reading your diary back at you.
