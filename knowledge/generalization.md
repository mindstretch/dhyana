# Generalization Layer

The corpus is written in the specific vocabulary of **Transcendental Meditation
(TM)** and **Maharishi Vedic Science (MVS)** — a trademarked technique and a
particular tradition. The product must speak universally: no trademarks, no
required teacher/mantra/initiation, no cosmology a user must accept to benefit.

This file is the canonical translation layer. Every decomposition applies it.

## The five rules

1. **Universal user-facing language.** The tradition is named only in an optional
   `lineage:` line, never required to understand the idea. A stressed
   product manager should get value without knowing what "Veda" means.

2. **Generalize practices — never generalize evidence.** A practice can be
   described generically. A *research claim* stays attached to exactly what was
   studied. If the study measured TM, the evidence tag says so. Generic practices
   earn their own evidence; they do not inherit TM's results by resemblance.

3. **Keep the experiential instruction, drop the doctrinal commitment.** Teach
   *what to do and what to notice*. Leave metaphysics optional. "Let attention
   settle until thinking quiets" travels; "experience the unbounded ocean of
   absolute Being" does not.

4. **No gatekept mechanics.** If a practice as-studied requires a certified
   teacher, a personal mantra, or paid initiation, the shippable version is an
   honest *approximation* — and is labeled as one, not passed off as the same thing.

5. **Preserve the rigor, not the branding.** The founder's intellectual honesty
   is the asset. Universal ≠ vague. Universal + precise + honest is the target.

## The silent-settling-practice caveat (read this)

The corpus's evidence is for **TM specifically** — a taught, mantra-based,
effortless-settling technique. Our shippable "silent settling practice" is a
*generic approximation* of that style. Two honest consequences:

- **TM studies are tagged `studied via TM`.** They support the *direction*, not
  a guarantee that a generic app practice replicates the effect.
- **Generic settling/meditation has its own, broader evidence base**
  (mindfulness meta-analyses, MBSR, etc.) that the corpus does *not* contain.
  When we lean on generic practices, we must bring that literature in — a
  research task, not a relabeling.

This caveat is the difference between a credible product and a wellness mirage.
